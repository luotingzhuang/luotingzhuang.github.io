## About Me

I am Luoting (Lottie) Zhuang, a PhD candidate in Medical Informatics at UCLA, expected to graduate in June 2027. I build machine learning methods for precision oncology, translational research, and clinical decision-making.

My work spans multimodal and vision-language foundation models, longitudinal disease modeling, imaging biomarkers, and clinical outcome prediction. I am especially interested in turning imaging, clinical-trial, pathology, and genomic data into reliable tools for cancer care.

**Technical Expertise:** Multimodal AI · Longitudinal Modeling · Generative AI · Medical Imaging · Interpretable ML
